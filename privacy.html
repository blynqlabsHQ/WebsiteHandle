Privacy

The room is heard, not kept.

Timing, not identity.

The microphone is there for timing, not identity. It listens for when someone is speaking so the glasses know to stay out of it. No audio is kept and no faces are learned.

What you asked and what you were told stays on your phone. It is the only way to check what a device that speaks actually said.

These are worn in public, around people who did not choose to be near a device. Building for that is not a compliance exercise. It is the reason the product can be worn at all.
